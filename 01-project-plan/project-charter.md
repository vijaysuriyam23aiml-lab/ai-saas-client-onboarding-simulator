# Project Charter: AI SaaS Client Onboarding & Implementation

## 1. Project Overview

**Project name:** AI SaaS Client Onboarding & Implementation Simulator

**Client:** Acme Support (fictional enterprise client)

**Platform:** AI-powered conversation intelligence SaaS platform

**Project type:** Enterprise SaaS onboarding and implementation simulation

**Simulation period:** September 16, 2026 – October 4, 2026

**Team size:** 2

**Project status:** Planning

## 2. Business Problem

Acme Support wants to use an AI conversation intelligence platform to analyze customer interactions, improve agent performance, and provide actionable insights to its support managers.

Successful adoption requires coordination between the client, implementation team, technical stakeholders, and business approvers. Missing data, delayed access, incomplete requirements, or unapproved configurations could delay the launch.

The implementation team will coordinate these dependencies and establish a controlled path to go-live.

## 3. Project Objectives

1. Complete client discovery and document business and technical requirements.
2. Agree on project scope, responsibilities, milestones, and acceptance criteria.
3. Track all implementation activities, dependencies, and blockers.
4. Establish a risk register with mitigation and escalation plans.
5. Validate sample conversation data and required integrations using approved test data.
6. Obtain client approval for configuration and user acceptance testing.
7. Complete go-live readiness checks and document the handover.

## 4. Project Scope

### In Scope

- Sales-to-implementation handoff.
- Client kickoff and requirements discovery.
- Stakeholder identification and responsibility assignment.
- Project timeline, task tracking, and milestone management.
- Technical dependency and access-readiness tracking.
- Sample call-data validation using fictional or approved test data.
- Risk, issue, and dependency management.
- Client communication and approval tracking.
- User acceptance testing coordination.
- Go-live readiness assessment and customer handover.
- Project reporting using Excel and a simulated Jira workflow.

### Out of Scope

- Developing or training an AI model.
- Building the SaaS product itself.
- Accessing real customer call recordings or personal data.
- Deploying integrations into a real production environment.
- Making claims about actual Convin customers or internal processes.
- Performing real client onboarding on behalf of a vendor.

## 5. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Client Project Sponsor | Business decisions and final approval |
| Client Operations Manager | Defines operational requirements |
| Client IT Administrator | Coordinates access and integration dependencies |
| Implementation Coordinator | Owns project tracking, communication, and escalations |
| Technical Implementation Owner | Coordinates technical readiness and testing |
| Customer Success Manager | Receives the account during handover |

The two-person project team will divide coordination and technical tracking responsibilities. Client stakeholder roles are simulated for this exercise.

## 6. Success Criteria

The simulation will be considered successfully completed when:

- All agreed project tasks have a documented owner and status.
- All critical dependencies have an owner and a target resolution date.
- High-priority risks have mitigation and contingency plans.
- Required client approvals are documented in the project records.
- Planned test cases have documented results and acceptance decisions.
- No unresolved critical blocker remains at the simulated go-live decision.
- A go-live decision and customer handover checklist are documented.
- The final project status and lessons learned are recorded in GitHub.

These are proposed project acceptance criteria, not actual customer results.

## 7. Key Deliverables

1. Project charter and scope document.
2. Implementation timeline and milestone plan.
3. Stakeholder register and responsibility matrix.
4. Excel implementation tracker and reporting dashboard.
5. Jira project and task workflow simulation.
6. Risk and issue register.
7. Client kickoff agenda and meeting minutes.
8. Weekly status reports and escalation communications.
9. Testing and acceptance checklist.
10. Go-live decision and customer handover document.
11. Final project summary and lessons learned.

## 8. Key Risks and Assumptions

### Initial Risks

- Client requirements may be incomplete or change during discovery.
- Technical access may not be available on time.
- Sample call data may be missing or unsuitable for testing.
- Client approvals may be delayed.
- Testing may identify issues that affect launch readiness.

### Assumptions

- The client has purchased the platform and assigned business and technical contacts.
- The client can provide approved sample data and required technical information.
- All implementation activities in this portfolio are simulated.
- Dates, responsibilities, progress, and outcomes will be documented as planned or simulated unless actual evidence supports a different status.

## 10. Project Governance

- Maintain one central project tracker.
- Review tasks, dependencies, risks, and issues regularly.
- Record decisions, action items, owners, and due dates.
- Escalate blockers that threaten agreed milestones.
- Obtain documented approval before marking acceptance milestones complete.
- Do not proceed with simulated go-live while a critical blocker remains unresolved.

## 11. Tools

- **GitHub:** Project documentation and portfolio version history.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Project tracker, risk register, and status reporting.

## 12. Project Completion

The project will be considered complete when the implementation documentation, tracking artifacts, risk records, test evidence, go-live decision, and handover checklist have been reviewed and published in the portfolio repository.

This project is an independently created educational simulation and is not an official Convin project.
